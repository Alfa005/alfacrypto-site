What is Chiliz?

Qu'est-ce que Chiliz ?

¿Qué es Chiliz?

これは何？

Being a sports fan has always been a one-way street: you buy the jersey, renew the season ticket, watch every match, and the club... doesn't know you exist. Chiliz flips that relationship. It's a blockchain platform that lets sports teams issue "Fan Tokens" — digital assets that give holders real voting power on club decisions, exclusive rewards, and VIP experiences. Think of it as turning passive fandom into active membership.

Founded in 2018 by Alexandre Dreyfus (the same entrepreneur who built Winamax, one of Europe's biggest poker platforms), Chiliz operates through its consumer app Socios.com. Fans use CHZ — the platform's native token — to buy Fan Tokens for their favorite clubs. These tokens aren't just collectibles: BAR holders voted on FC Barcelona's locker room music, PSG holders chose a Champions League message printed on team buses, and UFC holders influence fighter interaction formats. Over 70 major teams across football, basketball, MMA, and esports have launched tokens.

The Chiliz Chain — its own EVM-compatible Layer-1 blockchain — launched in 2023, cutting transaction costs and reducing dependence on external infrastructure. By March 2026, CHZ has a market cap of approximately $370 million and a circulating supply of around 10.27 billion tokens. The project is also one of the first major blockchain platforms to publish a MiCA-compliant whitepaper, positioning it well for European regulatory clarity.

The business model is clever: Chiliz takes a percentage of Fan Token sales and trading revenue, which flows back into the CHZ token economy through validator staking rewards and ecosystem development. Validators include major institutions like OKX, Animoca Brands, and even sports clubs like PSG — aligning ecosystem security with sports industry stakeholders.

How does it work?

Comment ça fonctionne ?

¿Cómo funciona?

どう機能する？

Chiliz Chain uses a Proof-of-Staked-Authority (PoSA) consensus mechanism — a hybrid of Proof-of-Stake and Proof-of-Authority. There are 11 validators who must stake CHZ to participate and are known, reputable entities (exchanges, sports clubs, blockchain firms). This creates faster block times (about 3 seconds) and higher throughput than fully permissionless networks, at the cost of some decentralization. It's a pragmatic trade-off for a platform prioritizing fast, cheap fan interactions over censorship resistance.

Fan Tokens are ERC-20-compatible tokens (on the Chiliz Chain, technically CFT-20) issued by sports organizations in partnership with Chiliz. Clubs set the total supply, and tokens enter circulation through Socios.com. Fans buy them with CHZ, and the token price then floats on the open market based on demand. Clubs periodically launch polls exclusively accessible to token holders — the more tokens you hold, the more votes you have.

CHZ itself functions as the gas token for all Chiliz Chain transactions. When users transact, fees are paid in CHZ — and 65% of newly issued CHZ goes to validators and delegators as staking rewards. The tokenomics follow an inflationary model starting at 8.8% annual inflation decaying to a 1.88% floor, with perpetual gas fee burns creating partial offset. This EIP-1559-style fee burning means transaction volume directly reduces net supply growth.

Cela crée des temps de bloc rapides (environ 3 secondes) et un débit plus élevé, au prix d'une certaine décentralisation.
